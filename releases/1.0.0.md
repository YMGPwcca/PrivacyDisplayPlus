## Privacy Display+ 1.0.0

The first public release brings Samsung Privacy Display to individual floating
windows and split-screen panes.

- Control floating windows and split screen independently.
- Choose window-only or full-screen privacy for individual selected apps.
- Protect an open window temporarily without adding its app to Samsung's list.
- Turn a floating window's protection on or off from its handle menu. A filled
  button shows when protection is on.
- Reload Android with the separate Soft reboot button on supported KernelSU setups.

## Requirements and setup

Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra SM-S948B, Android 17 / One UI 9, firmware
S948BXXS4BZIG / OXM4BZIG, and working LSPosed. Other firmware builds are blocked.

Install the APK, enable Privacy Display+ in LSPosed, select **System Framework**
and **System UI**, then soft reboot. Turn on conditions and choose your apps in
Samsung Privacy Display settings. The app shows **Active** when connected.

See [compatibility and limitations](https://github.com/YMGPwcca/PrivacyDisplayPlus/blob/main/SUPPORT.md)
before installing. There are two hardware privacy regions, and a small outer
margin is required for split-screen protection. Other Samsung conditions may
still protect the entire display.
