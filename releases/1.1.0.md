## Privacy Display+ 1.1.0

- Add Maximum privacy protection to per-app rules.
- Fix Maximum privacy failing when switching out of an app and back quickly.
- Match floating-window privacy to Samsung's rounded corners and screen-edge
  clipping while moving windows.
- Remove the redundant Protect a window screen. The native floating-handle
  toggle remains available.
- Correct broken punctuation in app text.

## Updating

Install the APK over the existing app, keep **System Framework** and **System UI**
enabled in LSPosed, then soft reboot once to load the updated hooks.

Maximum uses one level shared by all protected windows. When the last app's
Maximum request ends, the current Samsung level applies again.

The supported firmware and outer panel margins are unchanged. See
[compatibility and limitations](https://github.com/YMGPwcca/PrivacyDisplayPlus/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
