## Privacy Display+ 1.3.1

- Stop floating-window and split-screen privacy regions from lingering over
  Notification Center, Quick Settings, and the Recents transition.
- Restore the app's privacy region when the panel closes or the Recents gesture
  returns to the app.

## Updating

Install the signed APK over the existing app to keep your settings. Keep
**System Framework** and **System UI** enabled in LSPosed, then soft reboot
once to load the updated hooks. Recents cards themselves remain outside the
module's window-protection controls.
