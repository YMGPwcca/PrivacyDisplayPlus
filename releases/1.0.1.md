## Privacy Display+ 1.0.1

- Fix floating-window privacy disappearing near the panel edges.
- Keep protected regions aligned while dragging along an edge or moving back
  toward the middle of the screen.
- Restore normal region updates when a drag ends, is cancelled or is interrupted.

## Updating

Install the APK over the existing app, keep **System Framework** and **System UI**
enabled in LSPosed, then soft reboot once to load the updated hooks.

The supported firmware is unchanged. Window protection retains a small outer
panel margin: 14 physical pixels on the left/right and 16 on the top/bottom.
See [compatibility and limitations](https://github.com/YMGPwcca/PrivacyDisplayPlus/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
