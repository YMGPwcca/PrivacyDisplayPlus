## Privacy Display+ 1.2.0

- Add independent Off, On and Maximum privacy settings for Notification Center
  and Quick Settings.
- Replace the system-panel dropdowns with compact Material menus that show the
  selected option.
- Refresh App rules with matching menus and a grouped card for window rules and
  Maximum privacy protection.
- Follow the system's dynamic colors across the settings screens.

## Updating

Install the APK over the existing app, keep **System Framework** and **System UI**
enabled in LSPosed, then soft reboot once to load the updated hooks. Samsung's
**Conditions for turning on** must be enabled.

New panel settings default to Off. An existing Notification Center choice is
retained on upgrade. Maximum uses one shared display level across protected
apps and panels.

The independent Quick Settings and per-panel Maximum routes still need physical
device validation after reloading the hooks. The supported firmware is unchanged.
See [compatibility and limitations](https://github.com/YMGPwcca/PrivacyDisplayPlus/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
